Slow Flipper TV — in the browser

Send a picture as sound and receive it on another device or tab. Wire-compatible with the real Flipper Zero and Playdate: the transmitter's sound decodes on a real Playdate, and the receiver hears a real Flipper. Open two tabs (or two devices) to run the whole loop.

Transmitter

Flipper Zero. Load a picture, play it as sound through the speakers.

Receiver

Playdate. Listen on the microphone and draw the picture line by line.

Put the transmitter's speaker near the receiver's microphone. For two tabs on one machine, unmute the speakers and allow the mic.
