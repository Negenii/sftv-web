Slow Flipper TV in a browser

Send pictures and notes as near-ultrasound to a Playdate running SFTV RX, or receive what a Flipper Zero running SFTV TX sends. Two tabs or two devices run the whole loop.

Transmitter

Flipper Zero. Load a picture, play it as sound through the speakers.

Receiver

Playdate. Listen on the microphone and draw the picture line by line.

Keep the transmitter close to the receiver, 3–10 cm. Many laptop and phone speakers barely play 17–21 kHz: if nothing arrives, try headphones laid next to the receiver.
